Unified Omni-Channel Data Integration

The Problem

Sugar Cosmetics operated across 100+ retail stores with siloed inventory and order data. Real-time visibility was impossible, leading to overselling, stockouts, and fragmented customer experiences.

Design and Delivery

Assessed existing API capabilities across retail, e-commerce, and POS systems. Designed a webhook-based integration architecture for real-time inventory and order data sync. Coordinated technical requirements directly with the client's product team and validated the design through a staging pilot before go-live.

Impact

  • Scaled to 100+ stores across India with unified real-time inventory visibility
  • Reduced stockouts 35% through data-driven replenishment
  • Eliminated manual reconciliation (80% fewer touchpoints)
  • Freed ops teams for strategic work instead of firefighting

Amazon B2B Order Integration

The Problem

Manual order reconciliation across 250+ enterprise brands' Amazon B2B channels created operational inefficiency and error risk. No reliable sync existed between Amazon and Unicommerce.

Design and Delivery

Identified the problem and built the business case. Engaged directly with Amazon's team to scope available integration options. Partnered with engineering and leadership through solution design, recommending a polling-based sync model chosen based on Amazon's webhook limitations at the time.

Impact

  • Eliminated manual reconciliation across 250+ brands
  • Enabled seamless, scaled order processing
  • Reduced operational risk and human error
  • Freed finance and ops teams from routine reconciliation work

Ajio B2B2C GST Compliance Remediation

The Problem

Ajio's B2B2C model required two separate invoice chains. The existing integration mixed them, producing GST-invalid invoices. A client's CA flagged the mismatches, escalating to a compliance risk across 50+ brands.

Design and Delivery

Built the business case for remediation. Engaged Ajio's technical team directly to define the corrected invoicing approach. Partnered with engineering and business through the revamp, holding sign-off before rollout. Retrospectively corrected 5,000+ historical invoices with zero operational disruption.

Impact

  • Achieved 100% invoice validity post-launch
  • Retrospectively corrected 5,000+ historical invoices with zero disruption
  • Eliminated manual review, freed finance team
  • Reduced invoice-to-dispatch from 4 hours to 8 minutes
  • Scaled confidently across 50+ brands; zero compliance violations as transaction volume grew 3x

Filtrd: Ethical Investment Platform

The Problem

Women investors faced barriers: lack of accessible ethical investment education, limited Shariah-compliant options, and no aggregated financial data layer for third-party integration.

Design and Delivery

Translated market research (women's wealth gaps, Shariah-compliance constraints, investor sentiment) into a 0-to-1 roadmap. Built API layer enabling third-party financial data integration. Designed pricing model and community workshop framework connecting financial literacy to product adoption.

Impact

  • Designed backend infrastructure for scaled financial data integration
  • Built community engagement architecture connecting education to adoption
  • Established framework for workshop-led financial literacy
  • Creating infrastructure for ethical investing at scale

Sab Link: Artisan-to-Market Platform

The Problem

20+ artisans from marginalized communities had no unified platform for market access. Skill-sharing channels were fragmented. Artists had no aggregated visibility and faced barriers to direct-to-consumer sales.

Design and Delivery

Diagnosed the platform-level constraint: artisans needed both community visibility and market access. Designed a two-layer model combining skill-based workshops with a direct-to-consumer marketplace. Built the community layer first (monthly in-person skill-sharing workshops), then layered the digital platform enabling direct transactions and artist portfolios.

Impact

  • Connected 20+ artisan-businesses to market access and visibility
  • Built skill-based entrepreneurship framework for scaling
  • Created community-led model reducing reliance on single founder
  • Established sustainable pathway for minority group economic growth
